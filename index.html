Profile
There's no account here, and nothing to cancel — everything on this screen lives only on this device. Your data below is where you back up, restore, or permanently delete it.
Goals
Reference ranges
Values outside these ranges are marked in your Laboratory table. Defaults are typical adult reference ranges — edit them to match the ranges printed on your own lab report, since ranges vary by laboratory and assay. Marking a value only means it sits outside the range; it is not an interpretation.
Interpretation
Quick entry and document reading send what you provide — the text you type, or the PDF or image you choose — to be turned into numbers. With a practice code this passes through your practice's service to Anthropic and is not stored along the way; with your own key it goes straight to Anthropic. Either way your health record stays on this device, and nothing is saved until you confirm it. Leave both blank and everything still works — enter data directly instead.
Keys are issued at console.anthropic.com. Usage bills to your own account.
Your data
Everything is stored in this browser alone. Clearing site data, or losing this device, means losing your history. Export a backup regularly.
Signal Health Practice’s tracker is a personal tracking tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations, and does not interpret your results. Discuss laboratory findings and health decisions with a qualified clinician.
